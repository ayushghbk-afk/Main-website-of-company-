WE ARE TIRED — SEO STARTER PACK
================================

This ZIP contains three files for the root of your existing Cloudflare Pages website:

- llms.txt
- sitemap.xml
- robots.txt

UPLOAD INSTRUCTIONS
-------------------

1. Extract this ZIP on your phone.
2. Open the folder containing your website's index.html and assets folder.
3. Copy llms.txt, sitemap.xml, and robots.txt into that same root folder.
4. Upload/deploy the updated website folder to Cloudflare Pages.
5. After deployment, check these addresses:

   https://wearetierd.pages.dev/llms.txt
   https://wearetierd.pages.dev/sitemap.xml
   https://wearetierd.pages.dev/robots.txt

6. In Google Search Console, open Sitemaps and submit:

   sitemap.xml

IMPORTANT
---------

- This is an SEO add-on pack, not a replacement for your existing website.
- Keep your existing HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and assets.
- The sitemap uses the .html page paths used by your current multi-page site.
- If you rename or remove pages later, update sitemap.xml and llms.txt.
- llms.txt is an emerging convention, not a guaranteed AI indexing method.
- robots.txt permits general crawling; it does not guarantee that every AI service will crawl or use your pages.
- Search indexing and AI citations are not guaranteed.
