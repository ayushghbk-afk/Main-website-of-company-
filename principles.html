03 / Our principles

THE WAY
WE CHOOSE TO BUILD.

A mission only matters when it shapes everyday decisions. These are the standards we want every project to follow.

01 / ACCESS

Make useful things more accessible.

Where possible, build free or lower-barrier alternatives to useful services. Explain limitations and costs honestly rather than hiding them.

02 / ADS

Respect people's attention.

Avoid intrusive advertising, deceptive placements, and manipulative engagement patterns. Keep the experience focused on what the person came to do.

03 / PRIVACY

Collect less. Explain more.

Design with privacy in mind, ask only for information a product genuinely needs, and communicate clearly about data practices.

04 / OPENNESS

Choose open technology when it fits.

Support open standards, open-source projects, interoperability, and community contributions. Respect the licenses and maintainers behind them.

05 / LEGAL

Build alternatives legally and ethically.

Create original work, respect intellectual property and creators, and never treat unauthorized copying or access as a shortcut to our mission.

06 / QUALITY

Make tools that actually help.

Prioritize reliability, accessibility, clarity, and real usefulness over hype. A product should solve a problem without creating several new ones.
