01 / Our vision

A more open internet. For everyone.

Useful tools, knowledge, and digital experiences should be accessible—not unnecessarily locked away or buried under advertising.

We want people to learn, create, communicate, and get things done without barriers that don't need to be there. We’ll work toward that by building practical alternatives, one project at a time.

Read our vision ↗
02 / Why we exist

The internet should work for people.

We’re turning frustration into something constructive: useful products that respect people’s time, privacy, and freedom.

Our reason is simple. Too many online experiences ask people to pay more, watch more, or give up more just to access something useful. We want to make better choices possible.

Read our reason ↗
03 / Our promise

We’ll build every alternative we can—possibly and legally.

Not by copying what others own. By creating original tools, supporting open technology, and finding fair ways to make access better.
