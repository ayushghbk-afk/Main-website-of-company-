02 / Our reason

WE’RE TIRED
OF THE NOISE.

The reason behind this company is personal: we want a better internet, and we want to help build it instead of only complaining about it.

The frustration

Too many barriers to useful things.

A useful service can feel out of reach. A simple task can turn into a stream of ads. The experience can start serving the business model instead of the person using it.

We know that not every subscription is unfair and not every ad-funded service is harmful. But we believe people deserve alternatives—and more control over how they spend their time and money.

The founder’s reason

Turn frustration into building.

My reason for starting this is straightforward: I’m tired of seeing useful parts of the internet placed behind paywalls or interrupted by advertising. I want to build alternatives wherever it is possible, practical, and legal.

I don't expect one company to change the entire internet overnight. I want to start by solving real problems, making useful products, learning from people, and steadily expanding what we can offer.

The commitment

Build, don't break.

We will aim to create original alternatives, use open tools and licenses responsibly, respect creators and rights holders, and be clear about what our products do. Our mission is to expand choice—not to bypass legitimate ownership or access controls.

We’ll build every alternative we can—possibly and legally.
